Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

3H Group ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Crewly mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Full name, email address, and role (e.g., Owner, Supervisor, Accountant)
  • Hashed password (we never store plain-text passwords)

Work Data

  • Daily reports: attendance records, task descriptions, work progress notes
  • Material orders and purchase records, including receipt photos
  • Payment records and petty cash transactions
  • Project information: names, locations, budgets, timelines
  • Team information: team names, trades, site assignments

Device & Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Push notification token (for delivering alerts)
  • Network connectivity status (online/offline)

Photos

  • Photos you capture or select for work progress documentation and receipt images

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate your identity
  • To enable daily construction site reporting and team coordination
  • To process material orders, purchases, and financial transactions
  • To send push notifications about task assignments, verifications, and alerts
  • To synchronize data between your device and our servers for offline functionality
  • To generate cost reports and project analytics for authorized personnel

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers using MongoDB with encrypted connections. Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted device storage (Secure Store). Financial data (budgets, costs, payments) is access-controlled and only visible to authorized roles (Owner and Accountant).

The App uses an offline-first architecture — data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database and synced to our servers when connectivity is available.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Within your organization: Authorized users (Owners, Supervisors, Accountants) can view work data relevant to their role
  • Service providers: We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) for push notifications
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, regulation, or legal process

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and work data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. When your account is deactivated by an administrator, your personal data is retained for record-keeping purposes in compliance with applicable construction industry regulations.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, contact your organization's administrator or email us at the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Permissions

  • Camera: To capture photos of receipts and work progress
  • Photo Library: To select existing photos for documentation
  • Notifications: To receive alerts about assignments, verifications, and escalations
  • Internet: To sync data with the server

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or via email. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
